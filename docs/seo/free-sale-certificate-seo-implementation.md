## Objective

This implementation strengthens the Free Sale Certificate page for Google crawling, indexing, and ranking by aligning the page with high-intent search demand such as:

- `free sale certificate consultant`
- `free sale certificate consultant india`
- `certificate of free sale export`
- `FSC certificate India`
- `CDSCO free sale certificate`
- `DGFT free sale certificate`
- `free sale certificate for export`

The goal is to make the page a stronger match for exporters who need regulator-backed proof that their product is legally sold in India before entering foreign markets.
