## Objective

This implementation strengthens the Certificate of Origin page for Google crawling, indexing, and ranking by aligning the page with high-intent search demand such as:

- `certificate of origin consultant`
- `certificate of origin consultant india`
- `preferential certificate of origin`
- `non preferential certificate of origin`
- `dgft certificate of origin`
- `rules of origin consultant`
- `fta certificate of origin india`

The goal is to make the page a stronger match for both informational and commercial search intent while keeping it conversion-focused for exporters who need filing support.
